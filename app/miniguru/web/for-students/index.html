What can you do on MiniGuru?

  • Pick a project. Robots, circuits, games, art, craft, science tricks. You choose.
  • Plan it. Make your materials list. Many things you can find at home.
  • Build it. It is fine if it does not work the first time. Every try teaches you something.
  • Film it. A short video from a phone is enough.
  • Share it. Other young makers watch, rate and cheer you on.

Goins and levels

You earn Goins when your project is approved and when you help others. Goins are points, not money. They move you up the levels, from Sprout to Legend, and up the Ladder, a friendly leaderboard.

Be a guru to someone else

Help a friend, say something kind on their project and share what you learned. On MiniGuru, every child can become a guru to another.

Ask a grown-up first

Ask a parent or teacher before you buy anything or share a video. Never put your address or phone number in a video or comment.
